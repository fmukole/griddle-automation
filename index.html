Cape Town's gate automation and security team

Common questions

Your gate & fence questions answered

What services does Griddle Automation offer?

Sliding gate motors, swing gate openers, electric fencing, electric locks, CCTV, welding, plus installations and repairs of all of the above.

My gate has stopped working. Can you repair it?

Yes. Repairs are a big part of what we do. Call or WhatsApp us, tell us what the gate is doing (or send a photo), and we'll arrange to come and look.

Can you add a motor to the gate I already have?

In most cases, yes. We'll check your gate and recommend the right motor or opener for it, and quote you before we start.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the gate, the motor and the work involved, so we quote each job. Send us the details through the form or WhatsApp to get started.

Do you install electric fencing as well?

Yes. We install electric fencing and also repair existing fences, including energisers, wiring and insulators.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across the whole of Cape Town.

What are your working hours?

Monday to Friday 08:00 to 17:00, and Saturday 08:00 to 12:00. Messages sent outside these hours will be answered when we're back.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the quote form, WhatsApp us, or call 083 348 8690 or 061 924 6159. Tell us your suburb and what you need.
